Paired R² — Cross-Population Consistency

What R² measures here: For each pair of population sizes (e.g. N=10K vs N=1M), we take all 12 parameter conditions, plot the metric at NA vs NB, and compute R². R² ≈ 1.0 means changing N doesn't change which parameter combinations are best — the ranking of conditions is preserved. R² ≪ 1.0 means the best conditions at one N are different at another N, which would mean N must be considered in experiment design. If the entire heatmap is green (R² > 0.95), population size is truly irrelevant to the conclusions.
